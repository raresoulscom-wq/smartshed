What changed in 2025

Local Laws 48 and 51 of 2025 turned long-standing sidewalk sheds into a monthly cost. The city expects to start imposing penalties in early 2027.

  • Shed permits now renew every 90 days, and each renewal needs a progress report on the repair work.
  • The contractor holding the permit must keep a progress log, updated at least weekly.
  • After 180 days, the owner owes a monthly penalty for any month without work in progress, capped at $6,000 per shed.

How it works

No app to install and no new software for the crew. It runs in Telegram, which most phones already have.

  1. Sign up in two minutes

    Tap the link and give your company name. We find your active shed permits in city data, and you choose which ones to track.

  2. Your foreman sends a photo each week

    One photo per shed with a short note, like "repointing 3rd floor." Each entry is dated and saved to that shed's log.

  3. We handle the paperwork

    Three weeks before each renewal, you get a draft 90-day progress report for your engineer to review and file, plus alerts before every penalty date.
