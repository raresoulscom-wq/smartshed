What this shows, and what it can't

  • Sheds come from DOB NOW: Build approved permits. A shed whose permit is only in the older BIS system won't appear.
  • Local Law 48 penalties apply after a shed has been up 180 days, for each month with no work in progress: $10, $100 or $200 per linear foot per month for sheds up under 3 years, 3 to 4 years, and over 4 years, capped at $6,000 a month. DOB expects to start imposing them in early 2027.
  • Public data can't tell whether work is in progress, so the dollar figure is what a month with no work could cost. It's an estimate, not legal advice.
  • Shed length is rarely on the permit. When the job description states it we fill it in; otherwise enter your frontage.
